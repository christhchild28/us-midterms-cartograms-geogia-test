E-2026-001 · 2026-11-11 · Disclosed on page (S2)

Montana Senate map shows two of the candidates on the ballot

What was wrong. The page maps Seth Bodnar (independent) and Kurt Alme (Republican) only. It leaves out Democrat Alani Bankhead, Libertarian Kyle Austin and one write-in candidate. The headline share is therefore a share of the two shown candidates' votes, not of all votes.

What we did. Added a dated note at the top of the page that names the omitted candidates and says what the shares mean. The map data is unchanged. A full four-candidate version is planned.

How we will catch it. Every race must list its full ballot, and any omitted candidate must appear in a page note. A check for this is being built.
