E-2026-009 · 2026-11-12 · Corrected (S2)

Michigan governor: the ballot count was too high

What was wrong. Our note said 6 candidates were on the Michigan governor ballot. That count came from Ballotpedia. The Michigan Department of State's official candidate listing shows 5: Benson (Democratic), James (Republican), Hudson (Libertarian), Brandenburg (U.S. Taxpayers) and Campbell (Green). Both major candidates are on our map, so the map is complete.

What we did. The note now shows 5 and cites the Michigan Department of State. Map data is unchanged.

How we will catch it. Write-ins are never counted as ballot candidates. The state election office list outranks other sources, and any disagreement is logged.

E-2026-006 · 2026-11-12 · Corrected (S3)

Nebraska Senate: ballot count corrected again, from three to five

What was wrong. E-2026-004 said three candidates were on the ballot. Five were: Ricketts, Osborn, Robin Richards, Mike Marvin and Chuck Conboy. Marvin suspended his campaign but stayed on the ballot. The Nebraska Examiner and The Green Papers both report five.

E-2026-005 · 2026-11-12 · Corrected (S2)

Georgia and South Carolina Senate: ballot counts were too high

What was wrong. Our notes said 7 candidates were on the ballot in Georgia and 10 in South Carolina. Both counts came from Ballotpedia and included people who were not on the general election ballot. The Green Papers lists 2 for Georgia (Ossoff and Collins, plus 3 write-ins) and 4 for South Carolina (Andrews, Graham, Whitener and Hackett). The South Carolina vote shares add up to 100% across those four plus write-ins, so no other name was on the ballot. Georgia is now complete on our map.

What we did. Both notes now show the corrected count and the source. Map data is unchanged.

How we will catch it. When two sources disagree, the higher-ranked source wins and the other is logged. A state election office list will replace these when we can read one.

E-2026-003 · 2026-11-12 · Corrected (S2)

The "Votes cast" bar counted only the two shown candidates

What was wrong. The bar labelled "Votes cast" used the sum of the two shown candidates' votes as its total, so it read as a share of all votes cast. On 53 pages that is not what it measures.

What we did. Relabelled the bar "Votes for the two shown" on 53 pages. California, a top-two race, keeps "Votes cast". Every page now also has an "On the ballot" card with the candidate count. Numbers are unchanged.

How we will catch it. A label that says "votes cast" or "of the vote" must match its denominator. A check for this wording is planned.

E-2026-001 · 2026-11-11 · Disclosed on page (S2)

What we did. Added a dated note at the top of the page that names the omitted candidates and says what the shares mean. The map data is unchanged. A full four-candidate version is planned.
