E-2026-004 · 2026-11-12 · Corrected (S3)

Nebraska Senate: ballot count corrected from five to three

What was wrong. Our note and card said two of five candidates were on the ballot. A July Ballotpedia report listed three on the ballot after the Democratic nominee withdrew. A later Ballotpedia headline counts five names.

What we did. The page now says three candidates and names Mike Marvin as not shown. It says the count is being confirmed. Map data is unchanged.

How we will catch it. A roster with conflicting sources shows the lower confirmed count and a caveat until a certified list settles it.

E-2026-003 · 2026-11-12 · Corrected (S2)

The "Votes cast" bar counted only the two shown candidates

What was wrong. The bar labelled "Votes cast" used the sum of the two shown candidates' votes as its total, so it read as a share of all votes cast. On 53 pages that is not what it measures.

What we did. Relabelled the bar "Votes for the two shown" on 53 pages. California, a top-two race, keeps "Votes cast". Every page now also has an "On the ballot" card with the candidate count. Numbers are unchanged.

How we will catch it. A label that says "votes cast" or "of the vote" must match its denominator. A check for this wording is planned.

E-2026-002 · 2026-11-11 · Open (S2)

Eighteen more pages map only some of the candidates on the ballot

What was wrong. According to Ballotpedia, these pages show two candidates while more were on the ballot. Senate: Georgia, Texas, Nebraska, North Carolina, Kentucky, Michigan, Arkansas, Oklahoma, Florida, Iowa and Idaho. Governor: Arizona, Michigan, Nevada, Ohio, Wisconsin, Pennsylvania and Arkansas. Iowa governor is unresolved because Ballotpedia pages disagree. 33 other pages have not been checked yet.

What we did. Added a dated note with the count, names where known, and a source link to each of the twelve pages. Map data is unchanged. The audit continues; each result will be added here.

How we will catch it. Same as E-2026-001.

E-2026-001 · 2026-11-11 · Disclosed on page (S2)

Montana Senate map shows two of the candidates on the ballot

What was wrong. The page maps Seth Bodnar (independent) and Kurt Alme (Republican) only. It leaves out Democrat Alani Bankhead, Libertarian Kyle Austin and one write-in candidate. The headline share is therefore a share of the two shown candidates' votes, not of all votes.

What we did. Added a dated note at the top of the page that names the omitted candidates and says what the shares mean. The map data is unchanged. A full four-candidate version is planned.

How we will catch it. Every race must list its full ballot, and any omitted candidate must appear in a page note. A check for this is being built.
